Privacy Policy

Short version: your walking data never leaves your phone, and there's nothing to sign up for.

⚙️

Last updated September 10, 2026

🐀 Who this covers

This policy covers Rat King Recon, an Android game developed by an independent developer (contact below). It applies to the app itself and this website.

🚶 Step & activity data

Rat King Recon reads your device's step counter (Android's Activity Recognition permission) to turn real steps into in-game progress — Scrap, hatching, and EXP.

  • Step counts are read directly from your device's sensor.
  • All of it is processed and stored only on your device, in the app's local save data.
  • Nothing about your steps, location, or movement is ever sent off your device, to us or anyone else.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data. There is no cloud save and no account to recover it from.

📊 Crash & diagnostic data

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) to automatically report crashes, so bugs can get fixed. If the app crashes, Crashlytics may send Google:

  • A crash log / stack trace of what the app was doing.
  • Basic device info — model, Android version, app version.
  • A random, non-identifying installation ID (not your name, email, or account — there is no account).

This is the only data that ever leaves your device. See Firebase's privacy policy for how Google handles it.

🚫 What this app does not do

  • No account, login, or sign-up of any kind.
  • No ads and no ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases or payment data — nothing to buy, nothing to pay with.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any data with third parties, beyond the crash reporting above.
  • No location tracking (the step counter reports a count, not GPS position).

🧒 Children's privacy

Rat King Recon isn't directed at children under 13, and the app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age — see above, there's nothing to collect in the first place.
