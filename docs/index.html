The Loop

One walk. A whole workshop.

Every system in the app is built off the same idea — the steps you actually take outside power everything inside.

🚶 Walk → 🥚 Hatch → 📒 Collect → ⚔️ Fight → 🔧 Splice
🥚

Hatch the roster

Steps fill the egg. Every hatch rolls stats, rarity, and a shot at Shiny — with a premium Masterwork Hatchery for guaranteed top-tier pulls.

⚔️

Rustbot combat

Scrap-built enemies scale with you. Pick attack, defend, or special — and watch for the five Boss Rustbots hiding in the higher levels.

🔧

Splice & deploy

Fuse two rats into a mutant at the Splicing Vat, or send your roster on idle Scrapyard Expeditions and Ledger Tasks while you're away.

🏆

Achievements

A trophy case of boss badges and lifetime milestones — steps walked, rats collected, rarest hatches — all tracking what you've actually done.
